Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How far is Seema Resort from Mumbai and Pune?

We are right on the highway at Neral, about 1.5–2 hours by car from both Mumbai and Pune.

Is the resort near Matheran?

Yes. We sit at the foot of Matheran in Neral, a convenient base for the hill station.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Slot 1: 12:00 PM to 10:00 AM next day. Slot 2: 5:00 PM to 3:00 PM next day (AP meal-inclusive plan only).

Are meals included in the room price?

Meals are included only with the AP (meal-inclusive) plan, which covers an unlimited veg and non-veg buffet. Room-only rates do not include meals.

What room types do you offer?

Standard and Elite Rooms, Premium Deluxe and Couple Deluxe Cottages, Bathtub Suites, Family Executive Rooms, Studio Rooms, Quad Rooms and Dormitories. Call or WhatsApp us for the full list.

Is Seema Resort near Karjat?

Yes. Seema's Resort & Restaurant is on the Neral-Karjat Highway near Panchwati in Neral, so it is easy to reach from Karjat as well as Matheran.

Does Seema Resort take group and corporate bookings?

Yes. We host families, friends, corporate teams and larger groups. Call or WhatsApp us with your group size and dates and we will suggest the right rooms and rate.
