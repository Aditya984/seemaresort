Fine print

Terms & Conditions

  • A valid, government-issued photo ID is mandatory for all resident guests at the time of check-in.
  • Accommodation may be denied to guests posing as a "couple" if suitable proof of identification is not presented at check-in.
  • Early check-in and late check-out are strictly subject to room availability and may incur an additional fee. Please check with the reception desk for details.
  • All advances are non-refundable. However, bookings postponed at least 3 days prior to the check-in date can have the full amount applied toward a future stay (subject to room availability).
  • Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in, or failure to arrive (no-show), are strictly non-refundable and will result in the voucher being fully redeemed.
  • All special requests are subject to hotel availability at the time of arrival. These requests cannot be guaranteed and may incur additional charges.
  • Proper swimming costumes are mandatory. Nylon, Lycra, or Polyester swimwear is preferred. Acceptable attire includes swimming trunks, shorts, swimsuits, swim leggings, and rash guards. Infants and young children must wear proper swim diapers if required.
  • Pets are not allowed on the property.
  • Any damage caused to the property, rooms, or resort amenities by the guest will be evaluated and fined accordingly.
  • The amount paid for the room or covered by the reservation voucher does not include charges for optional services, extra facilities, or incidental expenses.
  • Guests requiring a GST invoice must provide their valid GST number at the time of check-in to obtain a GST-compliant bill at check-out.
  • Infants & Toddlers (0 to 5 years): Stay completely free of charge.
  • Children (5 to 10 years): An additional charge of ₹1,200/- per child applies.
  • Guests Above 10 Years: Considered adults for pricing purposes and will be charged the standard adult rate.
